Set Up

  1. Overview
  2. Four moments of alignment: humans, orgs, experts, machines At this point we have introduced our four objects of concern: human intelligence, organizational intelligence, expert intelligence, and machine intelligence.
  3. Alignment 101 : Brian Christian's The Alignment Problem Some concrete examples of what we are worried about.
  4. July 2026: the Pfizer Building Failure in New York and the OAI/HF "Incident" What's different about AI alignment problems? And preview of how to read hard stuff.

EARLY ASSESSMENT WRITTEN ASSIGNMENT

  1. Amodei et al. Five Concrete Problems avoiding side effects, scalable oversight, safe exploration, robustness to domain shift, reward hacking
  2. Solutions in Amodei et al. Drill down into Amodei solutions and introduce categories of qualification, safety, transparency, structures, control (and all of their manifestations)

TOPIC SELECTION ASSIGNMENT

  1. AI 101 Amodei et al. present some "what is that?" questions we need to answer what is a model? parameters & weights; neurons; layers; activation; supervised/unsupervised/reinforcement
  2. AI102
    From next word predictor to helpful assistant. Embedding. Pretraining. Supervised fine tuning. Personas. Raise question: so where does the alignment and values stuff come in?

Alignment Problems Are As Old As We Are

  1. Fine Tuning Humans - Sacred Texts as Alignment Mechanisms Partly provocative; partly laying out basic categories of alignment pathologies Also this is "shared meaning I"
  2. The Problem of Social Order: how social science formalizes problems sacred texts implicitly worried about Cooperation and Coordination. Free riders. Adverse selection. Information asymmetry. Power. Self interest.

Just Make the Agents Good Agents

  1. Ethics 101: can't we resolve this simply by creating GOOD agents (humans, orgs, experts, machines) what does field of ethics look like? normative ethics; meta-ethics; descriptive ethics; applied ethics
  2. Ethics 102: read Wallach and Vallor. Design perfect roommate/robot and see how practical wisdom is what we lack. (IRL gets snuck in here - we are thinking through our attempt to build values into a roommate or a robot and recognizing that there is more to it - they need to learn over time how to be ethically subtle - phronesis - and we ask about IRL - does it point us in direction of learning what "really matters" as opposed to learning a simple rule?)
  3. Evals: how well does that work in practice? In class exercise: Simple Eval Exercise
  4. Ethics exercise: would LLMs pass our ethics rubric?

ETHICS ASSIGNMENT: Can an LLM meet JHU WSE ethical reasoning requirement?

Is Value Alignment Enough?

  1. Values Aren't Enough I: Humans align via shared meaning (GHMead generalized other and CIRL)
  2. Values Aren't Enough II: Humans use markets (cf qualification-as-signaling and transparency-as-disclosure)
  3. Values Aren't Enough III: Humans build organizations (principal agent problems, incentives, deterrence) Kerr on rewarding A..., Hadfield et al. incomplete contracting and AI (qualification, structural constraints, and control in the monitoring/incentives/screening literature)
  4. Values Aren't Enough IV: Humans use groups and Norms and informal order (Centola, Ellickson, Köster et al.) (cf transparency-as-reputation and control-as-informal-sanction)

  2. Constitutions II: Claude constitution, OAI model spec, Caputo on constitutions
