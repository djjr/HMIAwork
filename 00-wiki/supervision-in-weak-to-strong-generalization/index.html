00 Wiki › supervision in weak to strong generalization

In supervised learning, "supervision" = providing the target labels a model is trained against. In weak-to-strong generalization (Burns et al. 2023), the weak model isn't watching the strong model, it's generating a set of labels on some dataset (e.g., "is this answer correct: yes/no"), and the strong model is then fine-tuned with ordinary supervised fine-tuning to match those labels. "Weak supervises strong" just means "the weak model's outputs are the training targets for the strong model's fine-tuning."
