Engage with an LLM

Present an "ethics scenario" to the LLM of your choice and grade the results. You are playing the role of a manager. Treat the model as a new analyst on your team who has handed you this analysis. In copying and pasting LLM output, remember our convention of making the text purple.

Preparation

Start in a new session with memory off. Make sure you have no custom instructions or personal preferences, and use a temporary or incognito chat where the product offers one.
