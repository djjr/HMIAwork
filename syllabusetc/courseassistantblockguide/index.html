Course Assistant (CA) Session Block Guide & Tracker

Role: Session Producer / Module Lead
Scope: Operational, editorial, and delivery ownership for an assigned block of 1–4 class sessions.

1. Onboarding & Fundamentals

Core Mindset

  • Act as a Producer: You own the student-facing execution and timeline for your block. The instructor supplies vision and subject matter and (most of) the content; you ensure it lands cleanly, consistently, and on time.
  • Permission to Nudge: You are explicitly expected to follow up on overdue slides, unfinalized notes, and missing rubrics. Polite persistence is part of the job.
  • Verify Every Asset: Never assume due dates, times, and syllabus text align automatically. Check each surface manually.

Initial Setup Checklist

  • Access permissions confirmed: LMS (Canvas/Blackboard), shared drive, slide decks, and gradebook.
  • Calendar mapping: Add session dates, milestone checkpoints (T-14, T-7, T-3, T-1 days), and assignment release/due dates to your calendar.
  • Communication channels: Slack first, email second, text message third, note tied to rock thrown through window fourth.

2. Block Lifecycle Checklist

Phase 1: Planning & Content Review (T-14 to T-3 Days)

  • Audit drafts & outlines: Review notes and slides for placeholder items ([TBD], [Insert link], incomplete sections). Remind instructor of his to-do list.
  • Verify links: Check that all reading URLs, library reserves, and DOI links resolve without paywall blockers.
  • Cross-check dates: Confirm dates on pre- and post-class work (due midnight before class, available from noon Thursday prior, available until 6pm day of), and verify this appears correctly on student facing LMS calendar.
  • Content nudge (T-10 to T-7 Days): Engage instructor in conversation about content. Flag confusing slides, propose concrete examples or diagrams, and note pacing/clarity concerns. Check in on remaining open drafts.

Phase 2: Launch & Live Delivery (T-24 Hours to Class Day)

  • Lock assets (T-24h): Slide deck visible at URL on Canvas? QR code on deck? All drafts and outtakes and archival material moved to back of deck or set to invisible? Embeds in deck all working?
  • In-class readiness check: If needed, verify breakout prompts, live polling, handouts, and guest speaker links are operational.
  • Initial feedback on submitted work: scan and respond to some early submits. If appropriate, tell instructor about things people haven't understood and things people hit home runs on.

Phase 3: Post-Session & Assessment Pipeline (Class Day to T+7 Days)

  • Post-class publishing (T+24h): Does anything need to be uploaded to Canvas (recordings, poll results, workshop outputs)?
  • Grading setup: decide with team what parts of submitted work will be graded and monitor progress.
  • Handoff & debrief: Log recurring student points of confusion; note anything next session producer should know.
