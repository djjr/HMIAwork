Discussion and Active Classroom

This will not be a straight up lecture course. We will endeavor to lean toward discussion, debate, and other forms of active learning. But a discussion involving 30 people means we don't hear most of the voices in the room, so we may often break up into smaller groups so that everyone gets a little more floor time and a little more peer interaction. Sometimes we will reconvene and "report out" from small groups and sometimes we won't, expecting instead that you reflect on the class discussion in PostClass Work (see below).

Be Prepared

To get the most out of our time together in the classroom, we expect everyone to be fully prepared for every class.

Instructions on what to read or watch before each class will be found in a Canvas LMS quiz called "PreClass Work." In addition to reading assignments (usually the full text will be embedded in the quiz), there will be STOP+THINK questions that you can answer and compare your responses to a model answer. These are not recorded and you can treat them as optional. But sometimes they will form the basis of discussion in the next class.

PreClass work may also contain multiple choice comprehension questions and reflection questions. Comprehension questions are worth zero points and do not affect grades. Reflection questions are worth 3 points and graded on scale of (0=not done, 1=below expectations, 2=meets expectations, 3=exceeds expectations. 3s will be rare. Full credit for PrecClass work is all 2s. No extra credit for 3s but 3s can make up for 1s.)

Many classes will also have a PostClassWork assignment. PostClassWork is a Canvas LMS quiz that asks you to reflect or build on the class session's material. PostClassWork scoring works the same as PreClassWork.

Late submissions for Pre and PostClassWork are not accepted for credit.
