SyllabusEtc › Thoughts about Classroom E etiquette

We have 75 minutes together, twice a week. I expect us all to treat that time as relatively sacred. The value of this time to each of us depends on everyone else. Your classmate's experience depends on you coming to class fully prepared to participate as yours does on them. Your experience depends on your classmates fully attending to the experience as does theirs on you.

In the typical classroom layout what you do on your laptop or tablet or phone is not easily surveilled by the instructor so you can get away with being digitally absent. But everyone sitting near and behind you is potentially distracted by your being distracted. I expect you to respect your classmates' right to not be distracted by you being distracted. And if I do notice that you're digitally absent, I might just ask you to leave.

If you have code to work on or assignments to complete for other classes please skip our class and work on it elsewhere. Any credit you get for attendance under such conditions is not really honest credit and it affirmatively affects your classmates' experience.

--Dan Ryan
